## Slide 1 — Title
**Teacher-Hub**  
AI tutor for grades 4–6 math

---

## Slide 2 — Problem
One worksheet for 28 kids.  
Stuck students wait for help.  
Content libraries don’t write practice for *this* gap *tonight*.

*Speaker note:* Make it human — “kid at the kitchen table still stuck.”

---

## Slide 3 — Solution (UC-1)
Stuck student → short quiz → find the gap → AI writes a worksheet → grade → level adjusts.

**Outcome:** the right practice, in minutes.

*Speaker note:* This is the only use case you must prove live.

---

## Slide 4 — Why not just Khan?
Khan picks the next item from a library.  
We **write** the next problem from a live student model.

*Speaker note:* Respect Khan. Differentiate on *generate + adapt this attempt*, not “we have more videos.”

---

## Slide 5 — Who buys / who benefits
**This weekend:** student experience (parents feel the pain).  
**Next:** teachers (differentiation / “why is Sarah stuck?”).  
**Later:** schools (outcomes + less prep cost).

*Speaker note:* Don’t list three equal buyers. Beachhead first.
